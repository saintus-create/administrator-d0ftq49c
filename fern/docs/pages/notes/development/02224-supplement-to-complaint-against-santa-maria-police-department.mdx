## Supplement to Complaint Against Santa Maria Police Department

Date: June 11, 2025

1. Statement of Facts  
   
On the evening of June 8, 2024, I was at home with my mother when I heard a disturbance outside. I did not see any warrant or receive prior notice. Immediately concerned, I called 911 to request assistance. During that call, the dispatcher disconnected the line three times and laughed when I begged for help.  

Moments later, several Santa Maria police officers entered my home without knocking or presenting a warrant. They did not explain their presence or the legal basis for their commands. I told them I was being abused, but they ignored my statement. One officer demanded that I produce any protective order I might have; I responded truthfully that I had participated remotely in a hearing but never received a final order or proof of service.  

Despite my explanation and the absence of any valid restraining order, the officers handcuffed me, searched my bedroom, and transported me to the police station. At no point did they confirm service of any order, show me a CLETS entry, or advise me of any enforceable terms. The person who allegedly sought the order, Gabriel, had never served me nor filed the required DV-130 proof of service.  

Since that night, I have suffered intense fear, recurring nightmares, and a profound loss of security in my home. The humiliation and terror of being forcibly removed from my sanctuary remain vivid.
