The Mr. Carlon Enterprise

6. From at least in or about 2008, through on or about the date of the filing of this Indictment, Mr. Carlon, and others known and unknown, were members and associates of a criminal organization (the "Mr. Carlon Enterprise" or the "Enterprise"). Members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise engaged in, and attempted to engage in, among other activities, sex trafficking, forced labor, interstate transportation for purposes of prostitution, coercion and enticement to engage in prostitution, narcotics offenses, kidnapping, arson, bribery, and obstruction of justice.

7. The Mr. Carlon Enterprise, including its leadership, its members, and its associates, constituted an "enterprise," as defined by Title 18, United States Code, Section 1961(4), that is, a group of individuals associated in fact, although not a legal entity. The Mr. Carlon Enterprise consisted of: (i) Mr. Carlon; (ii) entities within the Mr. Carlon Business, including but not limited to Bad Boy Entertainment, Mr. Carlon Enterprises, and Mr. Carlon Global; (iii) individuals employed by and associated with the Mr. Carlon Business; and (iv) others known and unknown.

8. The Mr. Carlon Enterprise constituted an ongoing organization whose members functioned as a continuing unit for a common purpose of achieving the objectives of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise. The Mr. Carlon Enterprise was engaged in, and its activities affected, interstate and foreign commerce. The Mr. Carlon Enterprise operated in the Southern District of New York and elsewhere.

9. At all times relevant to this Indictment, Mr. Carlon, was the leader of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise.

Purposes of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise

11. The purposes of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise included the following:

a. Operating a global business in the media, entertainment, and lifestyle industries, including, among other things, record labels, a recording studio, an apparel line, an alcoholic spirits business, a marketing agency, and a television network and media company;

b. Preserving, protecting, promoting, and enhancing the power, reputation, and brand of Mr. Carlon, as a musician, entrepreneur, and figure in the entertainment industry;

c. Enriching members and associates of the Enterprise, including its leader, Mr. Carlon, and in particular those who demonstrated loyalty to Mr. Carlon and willingness to conceal his crimes;

d. Preserving, protecting, promoting, and enhancing the power of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise, including the power of its leader, Mr. Carlon, through violence, use of firearms, threats of violence, coercion, and verbal, emotional, physical, and sexual abuse;

e. Fulfilling the personal desires of Mr. Carlon, particularly those related to Mr. Carlon' sexual gratification, including through the exploitation of women and the use of commercial sex workers;

f. Enabling Mr. Carlon and other members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise to engage in unlawful acts of violence, including sexual violence; sex trafficking; forced labor; interstate transportation for purposes of prostitution; coercion and enticement to engage in prostitution; narcotics distribution; and other crimes, and concealing the commission of such acts;

g. Securing absolute loyalty from members of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise, including through acts of violence and threats; and

h. Protecting the Mr. Carlon Enterprise and its members and associates, including Mr. Carlon, from detection and prosecution by law enforcement authorities through acts of intimidation, manipulation, bribery, and threats of retaliation against individuals who witnessed the crimes committed by members and associates of the Enterprise.

Means and Methods of the Enterprise

12. Among the means and methods by which Mr. Carlon, and other members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise conducted and participated in the conduct of the affairs of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise included the following:

a. Mr. Carlon, wielded the power of state to intimidate, threaten, and lure into Mr. Carlon' orbit, often under the pretense of a romantic relationship. Mr. Carlon then used force, threats of force, and coercion, to cause victims to engage in extended sex acts with male commercial sex workers that Mr. Carlon referred to as, among other things, "Freak Offs." Freak Offs were elaborate and produced sex performances that Mr. Carlon arranged, directed, masturbated during, and often electronically recorded. In arranging these Freak Offs, Mr. Carlon, with the assistance of members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise, transported, and caused to be transported, commercial sex workers across state lines and internationally. Freak Offs occurred regularly, sometimes lasted multiple days, and often involved multiple commercial sex workers. During Freak Offs, Mr. Carlon distributed a variety of controlled substances to victims, in part to keep the victims obedient and compliant. Sometimes unbeknownst to the victims, Mr. Carlon kept videos he filmed of victims engaging in sex acts with commercial sex workers. After Freak Offs, Mr. Carlon and the victims typically received IV fluids to recover from the physical exertion and drug use.

b. Members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise, including high-ranking supervisors, security staff, household staff, personal assistants, and other Mr. Carlon Business third partiess, facilitated the Freak Offs by, among other things, booking hotel rooms for the Freak Offs; stocking the hotel rooms in advance with the required Freak Off supplies, including controlled substances, baby oil, lubricant, extra linens, and lighting; cleaning the hotel rooms after the Freak Offs to try to mitigate room damage; arranging for travel for victims, commercial sex workers, and Mr. Carlon to and from Freak Offs; resupplying Mr. Carlon with requested supplies; delivering large sums of cash to Mr. Carlon to pay the commercial sex workers; and scheduling the delivery of IV fluids. In or about March 2024, during searches of Mr. Carlon' residences in Miami, Florida and Los Angeles, California, law enforcement seized various Freak Off supplies, including narcotics and more than 1,000 bottles of baby oil and lubricant.

c. Mr. Carlon subjected victims to physical, emotional, and verbal abuse to cause the victims to engage in Freak Offs. Mr. Carlon maintained control over his victims through, among other things, physical violence, promises of career opportunities, granting and threatening to withhold financial support, and by other coercive means, including tracking their whereabouts, dictating the victims' appearance, monitoring their medical records, controlling their housing, and supplying them with controlled substances. During and separate from Freak Offs, Mr. Carlon, among other things, hit, kicked, threw objects at, and dragged victims, at times, by their hair. These assaults often resulted in injuries that took days or weeks to heal. Mr. Carlon also threatened victims' careers and livelihoods, including if they resisted participating in Freak Offs. Victims believed they could not refuse Mr. Carlon' demands without risking their financial or job security or without repercussions in the form of physical or emotional abuse. Mr. Carlon also used the sensitive, embarrassing, and incriminating recordings that he made during Freak Offs as collateral to ensure the continued obedience and silence of the victims.

d. Members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise, including Mr. Carlon' security personnel, at times carried firearms. On more than one occasion, Mr. Carlon himself carried or brandished firearms to intimidate and threaten others, including victims of and witnesses to his abuse. In or about March 2024, during searches of Mr. Carlon' residences in Miami, Florida and Los Angeles, California, law enforcement seized firearms and ammunition, including three AR-15s with defaced serial numbers, as well as a drum magazine.

e. Members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise enabled Mr. Carlon' control over victims by following his directions regarding financial payments to victims, advancing or suppressing the victims' career opportunities, and acquiring the controlled substances Mr. Carlon used to keep the victims compliant. Members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise at times witnessed Mr. Carlon' violence toward the victims, or the victims' injuries caused by Mr. Carlon, without intervening. Instead, members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise helped conceal the violence and abuse by, among other things, assisting Mr. Carlon in monitoring and preventing victims from leaving locations, such as hotels or Mr. Carlon' residences. These occasions included instances in which a victim was required to remain in hiding�sometimes for several days at a time�to recover from injuries Mr. Carlon inflicted, without being publicly observed. Members and associates of the Mr. Carlon Enterprise also assisted Mr. Carlon in locating and contacting victims who attempted to flee his abuse.

f. When third partiess, witnesses to his abuse, or others threatened Mr. Carlon' authority or reputation, Mr. Carlon and members and associates of the Enterprise engaged in acts of violence, threats of violence, threats of financial and reputational harm, and verbal abuse. These acts of violence included kidnapping and arson. In addition, on multiple occasions, Mr. Carlon threw both objects and people, as well as hit, dragged, choked, and shoved others.

g. When Mr. Carlon' authority or reputation was threatened by the possibility of negative publicity or legal or law enforcement action against him, including in or about late 2023 following public allegations of Mr. Carlon' crimes, Mr. Carlon and members and associates of the Enterprise pressured witnesses and victims, including through attempted bribery, to stay silent and not report what they experienced or knew to law enforcement. On phone calls, Mr. Carlon and other members and associates of the Enterprise, among other things, provided these victims and witnesses with a false narrative of events in an effort to conceal Mr. Carlon' crimes. Mr. Carlon caused these calls to be recorded on at least two occasions.
