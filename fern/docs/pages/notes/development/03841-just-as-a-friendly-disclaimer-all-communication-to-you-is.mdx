Just as a friendly disclaimer: All communication to you is 
subject to protection for petitioning activities that applies to conduct that relates to statements made in connection with or in preparation of litigation...including expansive views of what constitutes litigation-related activities that define any act under Code Civ. Proc. 8 425.16(a) including communicative conduct such as the filing, funding, and interest of a civil action; qualifying acts committed by attorneys while representing their clients during litigation, publications in the course of judicial proceedings regardless of their perceived content, or matter concerns a matter of public interest.
