The problem is not merely that the burdens are unequal. It is that the Plan permits the Provider to maintain credibility while imposing substantial hardship on the patient  to rebut them.

Rather than proving medical facts, the process drifts toward requiring the patient to continuously disprove denials. This effectively transfers the labor from the physician to the patient.

The asymmetry becomes self-reinforcing because maintaining vague or weak claims can require far less effort and expense than mounting a proper defense against them. In practice, the respondent is often forced to finance the precision and scrutiny that the proceeding itself should have required from the outset.
