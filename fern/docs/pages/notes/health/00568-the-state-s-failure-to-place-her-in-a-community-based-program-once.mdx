The State's failure to place her in a community-based program, once her treating professionals determined that such placement was appropriate, violated, inter alia, Title II of the ADA. L. C.'s pleading requested, among other things, that the State place her in a community care residential program, and that she receive treatment with the ultimate goal of integrating her into the mainstream of society. E. W. intervened in the action, stating an identical claim. 

Analogously, the State's failure to append the patient's approved medications, that would inevitably lead to the placement in a community-based program, after his treating professionals determined that such medication was appropriate violated, inter alia, Title II of the ADA. The complaint here, states, among other things, that the State place her in a community care residential program, by virtue of the required medication and that he receive such treatment with the ultimate goal of integrating him into the mainstream of society. E. W. intervened in the action, stating an identical claim.
