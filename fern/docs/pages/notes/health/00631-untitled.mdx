Treatment: Vyvanse 60mg Oral Caps. The insurer denied: Vyvanse 60mg Oral Caps. The denial is overturned. The patient is an adult female diagnosed with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)-inattentive type. Comorbid psychiatric disorders include mild and recurrent major depressive disorder, generalized anxiety disorder with panic attacks and posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD). There is no report of a co-occurring substance use disorder or a general medical condition that is a focus of treatment, or a diagnosis of narcolepsy or binge eating disorder. The patient has been prescribed Vyvanse 60 mg (milligrams) daily targeting symptoms of ADHD (attention deficit hyperactivity disorder), which include attention and concentration problems, difficulty in completion of tasks, difficulty listening and following directions, and other symptoms consistent with the diagnosis. The appeal notes that the patient has failed treatment with Adderall, Ritalin, Concerta, Focalin, and guanfacine, as well as off label use of Wellbutrin.
