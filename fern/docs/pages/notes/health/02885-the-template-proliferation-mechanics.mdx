### THE LINGUISTIC CONTROL MECHANISM

The template does specific psychological work:

**"He [frequency adverb]..."** - Establishes pattern (not isolated incident). "Always," "constantly," "never" erase nuance and grey area.

**"He [verb] me [location/manner]..."** - Spatializes the abuse. Makes it feel omnipresent. "In public," "in front of my friends," "when I was vulnerable."

**"He [triangulation with third parties]..."** - The most insidious part. Positions the woman as isolated vs. a conspiracy. "He told his mother I was crazy," "He made his friends think I was the problem." Unfalsifiable. Creates permanent paranoia.

**"He [emotional claim that can't be disproven]..."** - "He enjoyed my pain," "He felt nothing," "He targeted me specifically because [I'm empathetic/successful/etc]." Mind-reading presented as fact.

### WHAT IT REPLACES

This template **displaces** older relationship narratives:

**OLD:** "We grew apart," "He wasn't ready," "We wanted different things," "I ignored red flags," "I stayed too long," "We both made mistakes"

**NEW:** "He's a narcissist," "I was targeted," "He's incapable of love," "He traumatized me," "I'm a survivor," "He's doing this to his new supply too"

Notice the shift:
- From mutual responsibility ? asymmetric predator/prey
- From breakup ? escape
- From ex ? abuser
- From sadness ? trauma
- From moving on ? "healing journey" (indefinite)
