The concept of faith in Christianity has deep roots in both biblical and theological traditions. Job and Jesus are portrayed as models of steadfastness, persevering through trials by looking ahead to salvation (Garrett, 1999). Faith is considered the fundamental Christian virtue and the path to a saving relationship with God (Dulles, 1996). The Bible provides numerous examples of resilience through faith, including stories of Joseph, Naomi, Esther, and Paul (Agaibi, 2018). This connection between faith and resilience, while ancient, has only recently been explored in psychological literature. Jesus' own faith is a subject of study across various New Testament texts and extra-biblical sources (Wallis, 1995). These perspectives collectively emphasize the importance of faith and persistence in Christian theology and practice, highlighting their role in overcoming adversity and achieving spiritual well-being.
