These e-discovery service providers, whether operating outside the law firm or as an internal e-discovery team, tend to view e-discovery as a task rather than an adversarial contest. Their emphasis is on cost savings, speed, efficiency, and accuracy of production, rather than aggressiveness or an assurance of litigation victories.44 In the interest of maximizing such efficiency and cost reductions, they are also generally willing to meet and cooperate with representatives of the opposing parties, whether lawyers or information processors, and to cooperate more fully with each other in the discovery process.
