The goal is to simplify a passage by applying sluicing (ellipsis of the clausal complement of a wh-phrase).
1. Identify the Antecedent: Locate a sentence that introduces an NP (Noun Phrase) or a known action/event, often containing an indefinite expression (the correlate).
2. Identify the Full Question: Locate the subsequent phrase that contains a full or implied embedded question whose content is derived from the antecedent.
3. Identify the Remnant: Isolate the wh-phrase that precedes the portion to be deleted. This is the only part that will be pronounced.
4. Apply Ellipsis (PF-Deletion): Delete the entire clausal complement following the wh-phrase. This operation is the act of sluicing. The elided material must be semantically and/or syntactically identical to the antecedent material.
Specific instruction: "The TP (Tense Phrase) to be deleted must be recoverable from the preceding clause, ensuring the semantic interpretation remains unchanged, even when the resulting form is superficially much simpler."
