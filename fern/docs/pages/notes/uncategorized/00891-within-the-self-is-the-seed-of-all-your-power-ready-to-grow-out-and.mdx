Within the Self is the seed of all your power, ready to grow out and manifest into the world. Within this seed lies everything you can be. It is the blueprint of who you truly are, rather than who you should be.

A relationship which you will build painstakingly over the days, months and years. The Self is entrenched deep inside that psychological tunnel. If you are receptive to it, if you have faith, it will communicate with you.
