Filing a false report that triggers an unauthorized law enforcement response exposes every occupant of the multi-unit property to unlawful state intrusion, privacy violations, and physical danger. When a fabricated emergency brings out-of-jurisdiction officers onto posted private land, the resulting breach violates the Fourth Amendment rights of all tenants simultaneously.
The individual responsible for the false claim  subjects themselves to severe criminal liability, including charges for filing a false police report, obstructing justice, and potentially reckless endangerment or civil rights violations for endangering the constitutional protections and safety of the entire residential community.
