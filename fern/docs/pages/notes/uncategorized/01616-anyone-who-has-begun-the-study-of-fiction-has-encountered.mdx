Anyone who has begun the study of fiction has encountered
terms like point of view, flashback, omniscient narrator, third-person
narrative. Narrative Discourse is invaluable because it
fills this need, in this case, for a systematic theory of narrative. As a most
thorough attempt to identify, name, and illustrate the
basic constituents and techniques, it will prove indispensable
to find in it terms to describe what has already been perceived but will
also identify to the existence of fictional devices which 
had previously failed to notice and whose implications they had
never been able to consider. Every reader of Genette will find
that he becomes a more acute and perceptive analyst of fiction
than before.
