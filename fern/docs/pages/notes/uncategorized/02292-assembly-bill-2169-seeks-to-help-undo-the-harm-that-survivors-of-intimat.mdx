Assembly Bill 2169 seeks to help undo the harm that survivors of intimate partner and sexual violence experience when they are criminalized as a result of their abuse. AB 2169 will ensure that survivors, are able to use vacatur remedies to eliminate the ongoing consequences of old, legally invalid convictions and arrests. I am a survivor of intimate partner and sexual violence
For more than 2 years, I was forced to engage in court services for my abuser under threat
of homelessness, incarceration and separation from family who depend on me. I was also stalked and sexually abused by my abuser. I was charged with two violations of violating a restraining order after being intentionally misled, and after my abuser emailed the police station and misled the police to become unnecessarily violent, amongst other things, that I had intentionally and willingly violated an order, when it was actually my abuser who forced me to feel that expressing care for someone else could lead to jail or death compelling me to stay. Nonetheless, I was still convicted. Due to this criminal issue that was a direct result of the IPV I was forced to
endure I remain at risk .
