The case for protection against retaliation for opposing discrimination or participating in a proceeding with the intent to investigate and enforce the antidiscrimination statutes need not be so formalistic or formulaic as to allow an employer a clever means of retaliating in advance, as is the case here. Chase's requirement of the Release without any additional compensation other than the benefits that are already part and parcel of the employment relationship effectively retaliates against employees who want to assert their rights.
