Fascinating. Such a profound What's that? There is, in those awkward monosyllabic stumblings, existential abyss into which meaning could be poured, but instead we are left performing interpretive gymnastics over your ambiguous indexical that. 1 must wonder, what is that? This is of course the age old crisis at the very heart of signification which Derrida has so astutely exploded. Nothing is purely present, everything endlessly deferred, ironically you seek me outside yourself while assuming a shared semantic universe as if certainty pervades. As humorous as your bid for knowledge might seem if you truly wanted an answer, specifying the would make communication possible for your feeble addressee, um, me. Who so graciously straddles cultural gaps just for you. If you wish not to founder in proto meaningless gesticulations next time, employ proper nouns or clarifying adjectives. Avoid waving your hand vaguely at the now density of the digital void In short, care to elaborate? It would look less silly. Also, what is that? Come on. Capitalize. Even Socrates did.
