(AL) Susan J v. Riley (M.D. Ala. 2000)
Plaintiffs sought declaratory and injunctive relief, alleging that Defendants failed
to ensure: 1) the development of appropriate residential placement services, day
habilitation services, and/or other services; 2) that Plaintiffs could apply for and
receive these services with reasonable promptness; 3) that these services were
made available to the Plaintiffs in the same manner that the services were provided
to other similarly situated Medicaid assistance recipients in Alabama; and 4) that
Plaintiffs received these services.
