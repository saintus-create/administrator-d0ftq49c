SO the Police Department. On that Thursday when they arrested me, I was in bed.  I heard a loud clattering coming from outside my bedroom door. I got up my heart quickly raced, as I thought my mother had fallen. The picture frame in the hallway had fallen off the wall due to the force and my therapy cat was hiding. My mother, an elderly person with high blood pressure and a heart monitor, went to the front door. She told me it was the Police. She could barely catch her breath. They kept banging on the door causing the walls to shake, relentlessly. Due to the amount of litigation abuse that Carlon had forced me to endure, and the fact that I was still in recovery from the years. I began to have repeated and unwanted flashbacks of times he would  belittle me. Sending me into a state of paralyzed fear,  worthlessness,  and suicidal thinking. I hoped to find out why they were there, so I contacted the Dispatch Department in an attempt to deescalate.  I informed the dispatch that the police were here harassing my mother, yet she completely sidestepped any of my questions by asking me insistently where I was located. She informed me that I had no other option but to speak to the police on their terms, and eventually hung up on me. I called again and asked if I may speak to the officer over the phone.  Because I still did not know why. She hung up on me again. I called back once more, and informed her that I do not appreciate being hung up on. I was so confused as to why the police department, wished to resort to domestic violence in order to carry out their work, in light of the fact that I was making good faith efforts to communicate in a civil manner.  My mom afraid, opened the door, convinced now that there must be something I did wrong to cause this type of domestic disturbance yet it was the police causing it and I was attempting to resolve, it.
