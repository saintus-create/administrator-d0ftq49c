I do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult. On the contrary, repay evil with blessing, because to this you were called so that you may inherit a blessing. 
To you who are listening I say: Love your enemies, do good to those who hate you, bless those who curse you, pray for those who mistreat you. Trust him. Peace I leave with you; my peace I give you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your heart be troubled and do not be afraid.
