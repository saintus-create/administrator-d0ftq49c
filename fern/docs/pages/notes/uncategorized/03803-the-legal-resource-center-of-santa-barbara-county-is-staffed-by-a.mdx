The Legal Resource Center of Santa Barbara County is staffed by a California licensed attorney, and open to the public on a first-come, first-served basis.�However, Santa Barbara County currently has several divisions and the Santa Maria Division does not have a licensed attorney available. A pro se defendant is entitled to access to the law library in order to prepare for trial.
