Gottman (1999) posits that resistance is difficult to
defuse in therapy because it is the product of an internal
working model of relationships that is a distortion of com-
ponents of the sound relational house. The notion of an
internal working model is consistent with attachment and
object relations models. Certainly, individuals with BPD
may be viewed as having distorted internal working models
with regard to self and relationships. He believes that resis-
tance arising from individual psychopathology is manifested
by the uninfluenced stable steady state of the individual, which
is highly negative in the first place, and by the distortion in the
way the individual influences and accepts influence. This is
consistent with the DSM-IV-TR criteria of �pervasive . . . insta-
bility of interpersonal relationships . . . and affect� (American
Psychiatric Association, 2000, p. 706). The distortion in the
sound relational house, negative stable steady state of the indi-
