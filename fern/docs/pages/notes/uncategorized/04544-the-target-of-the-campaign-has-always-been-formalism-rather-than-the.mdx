The target of the campaign has always been formalism rather than the formalities
themselves. The statutory requirements for formality served useful
ends. They took the vast array of testamentary things and channeled
them into a judiciail form that was readily recognizable as abuse, thus easing
the uncritical acceptance. By imposing a standard form on
testamentary writings, they enable family courts to identify
documents solely on the basis of readily ascertainable formal criteria, thereby permitting to proceed as a routine, bureaucratic process.
